## Sora Technical Background: Evolution from Model to Application

Sora's underlying technology is a diffusion transformer model that draws on DALL·E 3's success in image generation. Its core principle involves denoising video frames filled with "noise" to gradually generate coherent, clear dynamic images. In February 2024, OpenAI first previewed Sora's stunning effects and officially released the first-generation model to select users in December of that year. Although groundbreaking at the time, the initial Sora still had limitations such as inability to generate audio and occasional physical simulation distortions.

Subsequently, Sora 2's launch addressed the core pain point of missing audio and significantly improved video realism, making it ready for direct publication in many short video scenarios. This rapid iteration provides clear clues for predicting **Veo 4**'s evolution direction.

## Sora 2's Strengths and Limitations: The Springboard to Veo 4

To understand **Veo 4**'s mission, we must first examine Sora 2's current state.

**Sora 2's strengths are primarily reflected in:**

- **Audio-Visual Synchronization:** Capable of generating dialogue, sound effects, and background music that match the visuals.

- **Excellent Visual Quality:** Outstanding performance in lighting, reflections, and camera movements, with the ability to generate clips up to 120 seconds long.

- **Multi-Scene Stitching:** Built-in editor allows users to combine multiple clips into longer stories.

- **Reusable Characters (Cameos):** Users can insert their own likeness into videos, achieving preliminary character consistency.

**However, Sora 2's limitations are equally evident, and these limitations are precisely the fortresses that Veo 4 needs to conquer:**

- **Unstable Physical World:** Despite improvements, complex physical interactions (such as liquid flow) occasionally still "break down."

- **Imperfect Character Consistency:** Even with reusable characters, hairstyles, voices, or facial details may still drift slightly across different scenes.

- **Difficulty with Long Video Narratives:** Manual scene stitching cannot enable the model to truly understand the overall narrative, with continuity relying on human enforcement.

- **Insufficient Editing Flexibility:** When dissatisfied with generated content, complete regeneration is often required, lacking fine-tuning capabilities.

These unfinished aspects are precisely why **Veo 4** is held in such high expectations. **Veo 4**'s mission is not just to generate cooler clips, but to become a truly reliable, controllable creative tool.

## Veo 4 Predicted Features: The Leap from "Generation" to "Direction"

Our core prediction for **Veo 4** is that it will achieve a qualitative transformation from "generating impressive content" to "building works with clear intent." Here are several major feature upgrades most likely to appear in **Veo 4**:

![Futuristic AI interface symbolizing Veo 4's leap](https://cdn.gooo.ai/gen-images/b7ff5dbd19e32b81375b573d98004a097b624cddb6faac79cd13143a00fb02f4.png)

1. **Longer and More Coherent Narrative Capability:** This is the most widespread expectation for **Veo 4**. **Veo 4** is expected to generate continuous footage lasting several minutes, with built-in understanding of scene transitions, pacing, and narrative flow. Users may be able to directly define scenes, rhythm, and transitions through scripts rather than manual stitching.

2. **Absolutely Stable Character Memory:** **Veo 4** is expected to introduce an "intelligent character memory" feature, ensuring a character's appearance, voice, and personality remain absolutely consistent throughout a project, completely solving Sora 2's "character drift" problem. This will be **Veo 4**'s key step toward professional film and television production.

3. **Fine-Grained Post-Production Editing and Real-Time Control:** Another key prediction about **Veo 4** is stronger editing capabilities. Users may be able to perform "regional editing" (modifying only part of the frame), replace objects, adjust lighting or duration without regenerating entire clips. More visionary predictions point to a mode called "Sora Live," where creators can issue real-time commands like directors on set ("slow down the shot," "switch to night scene"), and **Veo 4** updates the visuals instantly.

4. **Higher Resolution and Stronger Physical Simulation:** To meet professional needs, **Veo 4**'s output resolution is expected to reach 4K or higher. Meanwhile, its physics engine will be more refined, greatly reducing "AI weirdness" and making object behavior closer to the real world.

5. **Deep Audio Integration:** **Veo 4**'s audio capabilities will far exceed Sora 2. We can expect more precise lip-sync, character-specific voices, scene-aware spatial audio effects, and even the ability to generate dubbed versions in multiple languages from a single script.

6. **Seamless Integration with Professional Workflows:** **Veo 4** will likely provide APIs, deeply integrating with ChatGPT, Adobe Premiere, and other professional editing software, supporting team collaboration and becoming a powerful component of existing creative workflows.
